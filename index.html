Instructions

  • Print out the grid pattern on paper
  • Fill in cells with a pen to activate sounds at those steps
  • For instruments with two sounds (like HAT CLOSED-OPEN):
    • Draw a vertical line (|) for the first sound (e.g., CLOSED)
    • Draw a horizontal line (—) for the second sound (e.g., OPEN)
  • Position your paper in front of the camera
  • Press "Calibrate" to detect the grid
  • Press "Start" to begin playback
